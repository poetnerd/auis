It’s about Time

Our album Time drops Sept 28!

That night, we’re celebrating with an album release show at TCAN.

It wouldn't be the same without you!

You know the excitement you feel when you think about an upcoming vacation, or favorite holiday, or the return of the McRib™ sandwich?!?!? Well, that's the vibe here at FOCS central.


Time is a collection of tunes that takes the listener on a musical toboggan ride, from the grass-is-greener-ness of youth, through the self-doubt and second-guessing-ness as we mature, and into the panoramic perspective-ness one enjoys, both reminiscing and looking ahead to the back nine of this metaphorical round of golf we call life.


We'll be singing the album in its entirety, as well as some of our favorite cover material. Join us. No prior tobogganing experience necessary!
